TEST ACCOUNT
Use a dedicated Peppy Hosted test account without a Stripe or Google Play subscription, complimentary plan, or beta seat. Before purchasing, have the Peppy administrator confirm tester-list membership for the verified email used by Peppy sign-in, including an Apple private-relay address if you chose Hide My Email. TestFlight purchases use Apple's sandbox; Peppy accepts them only for invited testers.

SIGN-IN AND PLANS
Test Apple sign-in, then Google sign-in with a separate authorized account. In Hosted setup, check that monthly and yearly plans load their App Store prices, renewal terms, Terms of use and Privacy policy. Cancel one purchase prompt and check that you can try again. Complete a sandbox purchase and confirm that Hosted setup can continue.

RESTORE
Use the same Peppy account and purchasing Apple Account. On the Hosted subscription screen, select Restore purchases. Check the resulting plan access, not just the completion message. If an active plan takes you past that screen, record that case for a separate restore test. Do not delete account data, reset Keychain items, or cancel unrelated subscriptions to expose the screen.

STORAGE
With an active Hosted plan and a configured vault, open Settings > Account > Account & billing > Hosted attachment storage > Add more storage. Check all five tiers (+100 to +500 GB), their monthly App Store prices, Choose, and Manage App Store subscriptions. Tiers replace rather than stack; a smaller size takes effect at the next renewal. Test a sandbox storage purchase and tier change; check the allowance after refresh and relaunch, allowing for that downgrade timing.

REPORT
Send the build number, steps and visible error through TestFlight feedback. Do not include passwords, vault key phrases, purchase receipts or personal conversations. These are acceptance tests to perform, not claims that the flows have passed.
