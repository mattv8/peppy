TEST ACCOUNTS
Beta seat holders already have Peppy Hosted free: skip purchases. Purchase testing is only for testers asked by the Peppy administrator. Use a separate account on the tester list, without a Stripe or Google Play subscription, complimentary plan, or beta seat. Ask the administrator to confirm the verified email used for Peppy sign-in is on the list, including your Apple private-relay address if you chose Hide My Email. TestFlight purchases use Apple's sandbox.

SIGN-IN AND SYNC
Test Apple sign-in and Google sign-in with separate authorized accounts. Set up a vault and check that changes sync between devices.

PLANS AND RESTORE
If asked to test purchases, check monthly and yearly App Store prices, renewal terms, Terms of use and Privacy policy. Cancel a purchase prompt and try again, then complete a sandbox purchase. Using the same Peppy account and purchasing Apple Account, select Restore purchases on the Hosted subscription screen and check the resulting plan access. If an active plan skips that screen, report it for a separate restore test. Do not delete account data, reset Keychain items, or cancel unrelated subscriptions.

STORAGE
With an active Hosted plan and configured vault, open Settings > Account > Account & billing > Hosted attachment storage > Add more storage. Check all five tiers (+100 to +500 GB), prices, Choose, and Manage App Store subscriptions. If asked to test purchases, try a sandbox storage purchase and tier change; check the allowance after refresh and relaunch. Tiers replace rather than stack; a smaller size takes effect at the next renewal.

REPORT
Send the build number, steps and visible error through TestFlight feedback. Do not include passwords, vault key phrases, purchase receipts or personal conversations. These are checks to perform, not claims that the flows have passed.
